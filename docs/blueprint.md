# **App Name**: AquaMart

## Core Features:

- User Authentication: Secure customer, vendor, and driver login/registration, including password management.
- Product Catalog & Search: Customers can browse and search for bags of water and bottled water from various vendors.
- Vendor Storefronts: Each vendor has a dedicated page showcasing their available products and store information for customer viewing.
- Shopping Cart & Checkout: Customers can add water products to a cart, manage quantities, and complete orders with payment processing.
- Vendor Product Management: A dedicated interface for vendors to add, edit, update inventory for, and remove their water product listings.
- Order Fulfillment Dashboard: A dedicated interface for vendors to view incoming orders, update their status (e.g., pending, preparing, dispatched, delivered), and manage order details.
- AI Product Description Tool: Vendors can use an AI tool to generate clear and compelling descriptions for their water products based on key features.
- Driver Delivery Management: A dedicated interface for drivers to view assigned orders, update delivery status, access customer location, and communicate with customers.

## Style Guidelines:

- The chosen visual scheme is light, evoking clarity and freshness, aligned with the nature of drinking water. The primary color is a clean and vibrant mid-tone blue (#26A3DB), reminiscent of clear water. It has enough saturation and brightness to stand out against a light background without being overwhelming.
- The background color is a very light and desaturated cool grey-blue (#E8F1F5). Its hue aligns with the primary color, creating a harmonious and expansive feel, while its high lightness ensures readability and a sense of cleanliness.
- The accent color is a rich, slightly darker turquoise (#139489). This analogous color provides a complementary contrast to the primary blue, drawing attention to calls-to-action or important highlights without clashing. Its lower lightness and higher saturation make it distinctive.
- The main font for both headlines and body text is 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif. Its modern, objective, and highly legible design ensures clear communication, which is vital for product details and order information, while maintaining a clean, contemporary aesthetic appropriate for a digital marketplace.
- Utilize crisp, line-based icons that visually represent themes of water, delivery, shopping carts, and vendor management, ensuring immediate understanding and a consistent, clean aesthetic. Prioritize simplicity and clarity in icon design.
- Adopt a clean, grid-based layout for product displays and vendor listings, allowing for easy scanning and comparison. Information hierarchy should be clear, using whitespace effectively to prevent visual clutter and guide the user's eye, with a strong focus on mobile responsiveness.
- Incorporate subtle and smooth animations for interactions like adding items to a cart, transitioning between product views, and status updates within the vendor dashboard.
